01 Mark & wordmark

{{ readFile "static/img/logos/ocean-metrics-mark.svg" | safeHTML }}

The ensō — a brush-drawn ring rising like a sun over a horizon waterline. The ring inherits the surrounding text color (deep teal on light, sky-teal on dark); the waterline is always Sun yellow.

{{ readFile "static/img/logos/ocean-metrics-mark.svg" | safeHTML }}OceanMetrics

The logotype pairs two textures: “Ocean” in Fraunces (a fluid display serif — water) and “Metrics” in IBM Plex Mono (measured data). The brand thesis in one word pair, and the sole exception to the one-display-voice rule.

A product of the studio names itself after the wordmark: mark · Ocean Metrics · product (e.g. Places). Never recolor the waterline, put the ring on a busy photo, or set “Ocean Metrics” in a single face.

The Rationed Sun Rule. Yellow is a sunrise, not a coat of paint: at most one warm element per view. Two yellows in one viewport means one is wrong.
Teal carries the rest. Brand, links, buttons, focus — all teal. Yellow never takes a job teal should do, and never carries white text (dark ink only).
Both themes or neither. Every color ships a light and a dark value through light-dark(). A hex that only works in one theme breaks the toggle.

03 Typography

Display — Space Grotesk 700Measuring the ocean
Body — IBM Plex Sans 400Rigorous, reproducible analyses, maps, and open-data platforms for ocean programs.
Data — IBM Plex Mono 50035.4164°N 121.3568°W · NMS:PMNM · n=14,734
Wordmark only — Fraunces 600Ocean
Mono means data. IBM Plex Mono is for things literally measured or coded — coordinates, ids, counts, dates, labels. Prose is never mono; mono is never decorative.
One display voice. All headings are Space Grotesk. Fraunces appears in the wordmark’s “Ocean” and nowhere else.

04 Light & dark

Both themes are first-class: light is sunlit shallows, dark is the same ocean after dusk. The OS preference is the default; an explicit choice persists as localStorage['om-theme'], lands on <html data-theme> before first paint (an inline head script — no flash), and a 2.15 rem hairline toggle in the header flips it. Surfaces are flat at rest and lift on interaction. Try the toggle above — this page obeys it.
