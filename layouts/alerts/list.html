Feeds

The same changes are published as Atom feeds, for a feed reader or a script. No account, no address.

  • The list of feeds, one entry per feed with its title and address:
    {{ $feeds }}index.json
  • One place, by its place id:
    {{ $feeds }}place=<place id>.xml
    The example, the Channel Islands National Marine Sanctuary (NMS:CINMS): place=NMS:CINMS.xml
  • Everything, a collection or a place set: all.xml, coll=<slug>.xml (for example coll=places.xml, the sanctuaries) and set=<id>.xml.

A character in an id other than a letter, digit or one of : . _ - becomes _ in the file name. The addresses above are the path-style S3 address, which works today. The feed list names the feeds on storage.oceanmetrics.io (for example {{ $target }}place=NMS:CINMS.xml); that host serves them after the storage cutover, and until then it redirects or does not answer, so use the S3 address now and switch when it answers.
