The OBIS biodiversity-by-hexagon product (`obis-h3`) refreshed to **v20261101**: 164,627,996 OBIS
occurrence records joined to the World Register of Marine Species and summarized on H3 hexagons at
several resolutions, 236,073 files and 2.0 GB, served from storage.oceanmetrics.io and mirrored to S3.

The October build (`obisindicators` 0.12) had added per-resolution species tables so the atlas can
read a cell's species list at any zoom. The November publish also fixed a mirror job that had been
deleting files mid-publish; the mirror now only copies.
