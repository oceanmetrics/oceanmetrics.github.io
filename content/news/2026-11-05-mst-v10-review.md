Each new version of the Marine Sensitivity Toolkit now goes through a review gate before it is
public. **Version 10** was published from the Mac mini to S3 and the registry as a restricted
release, and deployed to the review host behind a sign-in; the check found the restricted layers
locked and the public ones open, and version 7 remains the public default.

Version 11, which adds the 2023 Atlantic seabird models and a revised share basis, is rebuilding
behind it. October's work came to about 370 commits across six repositories.
