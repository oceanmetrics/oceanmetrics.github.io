The MBON Biodiversity Indicators working group's manuscript, *Coding Ocean Indicators: Patterns
and People*, first submitted in June 2026, was revised in response to review and resubmitted on
5 November. The revision is tighter (the reviewers asked for a shorter paper) and carries a
point-by-point response, nine figures and three supplementary sections.
