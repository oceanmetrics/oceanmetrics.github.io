The CalCOFI integrated database now ships about once a month, and the November release is out:
**v2026.11.05**, 26 tables and 385,903,651 rows in 2.8 GB of versioned Parquet, built and tested on
the same pipeline as the staging run (106 release tests passing).

The CTD cast and derived tables were re-ingested, the Farallon bird and mammal transects joined the
release, and the hex-map, cruise and CTD apps, the R and Python packages and the documentation were
all redeployed against the new version. The release carries DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23993413.
