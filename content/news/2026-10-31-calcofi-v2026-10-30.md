While piloting an AI-readiness benchmark on the database, one cruise refused to line up: the July
2009 CTD casts carried a key that did not match their cruise. The defect was traced through the
ingest, fixed in the database builder, the workflows and the docs, and shipped as release
**v2026.10.30** with 20 datasets.

The release was tagged on GitHub and minted DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23833029; the notes, the catalog and
`versions.json` on storage.calcofi.io were re-rendered to carry it.
